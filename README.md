***🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI***

**📌 Project Overview**

Developed an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional business performance. The dashboard provides meaningful insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and dynamic filters to support data-driven decision-making.

**🎯 Project Objectives**
Analyze overall sales and profit performance.

Identify top-performing products and product categories.

Compare sales and profitability across different platforms.

Track monthly sales and profit trends.

Analyze country-wise sales distribution and performance.

Monitor key business metrics through interactive dashboards.

**📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
Total Sales

Gross Profit

Profit Margin

Units Sold

Total Orders

**📈 Dashboard Features**

Global Sales Distribution: Visualized sales distribution across countries using a map.

Sales by Category: Analyzed revenue contribution from different product categories.

Sales and Profit by Platform: Compared performance across shopping platforms.

Monthly Sales and Profit Trends: Tracked monthly business performance.

Top 10 Products by Sales: Identified the highest-revenue-generating products.

Country Performance: Compared orders, revenue, and profit across countries.

Interactive Filters: Added Year-Month, Country, and Platform slicers for dynamic analysis.
